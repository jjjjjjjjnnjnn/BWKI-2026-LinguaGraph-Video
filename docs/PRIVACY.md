## 2. Human Subjects (N=15) — GDPR Compliance

### 2.1 Legal Basis
- **GDPR Article 6(1)(a)**: Explicit consent (`Einwilligung`) obtained from all 15
  participants before data collection
- **No special categories** (Art. 9) processed: no health, biometric, ethnic, religious,
  political, trade union, sexual orientation data

### 2.2 What Data Was Collected
For each of the 15 participants:
- **Likert-scale responses** to language-specific prompts (German / English containing
  pairs)
- **Response timestamps** (no personally identifying metadata)
- **No** name, email, IP address, demographic data, or other PII

### 2.3 What Was NOT Collected
- No biometric data
- No biometric recordings (audio / video)
- No location data
- No device identifiers
- No cookies / tracking
- No third-party data sharing

### 2.4 Anonymisation (Art. 5 Pseudonymisation)
- Each participant assigned a **random numeric ID** (e.g. `P03`, `P11`)
- All responses stored under the numeric ID only
- The mapping table (ID → real identity) is held **only by the author** and **not published**
- The mapping was destroyed on 2026-08-15 (after paper submission) per the data minimisation
  principle

### 2.5 Storage & Security (Art. 32)
- Raw responses: encrypted local disk only
- No cloud upload of raw human data
- Aggregated statistics (LDS-C values, p-values) published in
  `BWKI-2026-备战/data/lds_c/`
- Access control: only the author (Jiajun Rong) has read access

### 2.6 Retention Period (Art. 5(1)(e))
- **Aggregated data**: Indefinite (for research reproducibility)
- **Raw individual responses**: Retained for 24 months from collection (2026-08-15) for
  verification purposes, then securely deleted
- **Mapping table (ID ↔ identity)**: Destroyed on 2026-08-15

### 2.7 Data Subject Rights (Art. 15–22)
All 15 participants were informed of their rights at consent:
- **Right of access** (Art. 15): participants can request their individual responses
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16): not applicable (responses are immutable)
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17): participants can request deletion of their individual
  responses; no such request received as of 2026-09-12
- **Right to restriction** (Art. 18): not applicable
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20): participants can request a copy of their
  responses in CSV format
- **Right to object** (Art. 21): participants can withdraw consent; no such request
  received as of 2026-09-12

To exercise any of these rights, contact the author via GitHub Issues.

### 2.8 Result in the Video
- The video **discloses** that N=15 showed no language signal under between-subject
  conditions
- This result is **explicitly framed as a design artifact, not as counter-evidence** (see
  `docs/faktencheck.md` § "Verbotene Behauptungen")
- The author followed the German Psychological Society (DGPs) and DFG ethics guidelines for
  minimal-risk student research

## 5. No Children Below 16

The N=15 participants were all **18 years or older** (recruited from a university-level
language class at Privatschule Schloss Heessen). No data from children below 16 was
collected (GDPR Article 8 special protection does not apply).

No international transfers of personal data occurred. The raw responses remained on
the author's encrypted local disk in Germany throughout the project lifecycle.
